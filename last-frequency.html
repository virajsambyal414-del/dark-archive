DARK ARCHIVE / FILE NO. 016

THE LAST FREQUENCY

A FICTIONAL TRANSMISSION FROM THE EDGE OF THE MAP

ARCHIVE CLASSIFICATION: FICTION / SIGNAL LOG
SUBJECT: A remote broadcast
STATUS: FICTIONAL CASE FILE
LOCATION: UNKNOWN STATION
ORIGIN: DARK ARCHIVE ORIGINAL

01. THE LOG ENTRY

The final page of the station log contained a frequency, a date and three words: “Still receiving clearly.” There was no sender name and no note explaining who had written it.

02. THE QUIET CHANNEL

For years, the station had monitored ordinary broadcasts: weather updates, navigation messages and routine test tones. This channel was different only because the log gave it a title: THE LAST FREQUENCY.

03. THE MISSING CONTEXT

The archive held no recording, only a handwritten entry. Without audio, equipment records or a confirmed time source, investigators could not reconstruct what had been received.

04. WHAT A RECORD CAN PROVE

A note can show that someone wrote something down. It cannot, by itself, prove that a transmission came from a distant place or that its words had a hidden meaning.

05. FINAL RECORD

The station was eventually closed. The frequency remains in the fictional archive as a reminder that every mystery needs evidence, and every missing detail leaves room for imagination.
