DARK ARCHIVE / FILE NO. 014

THE EMPTY ROOM

A SHORT FICTIONAL CASE ABOUT A ROOM WITH NO RECORD

ARCHIVE CLASSIFICATION: FICTION / MYSTERY
SUBJECT: An unlisted room
STATUS: FICTIONAL STORY
LOCATION: ARCHIVE BUILDING — FLOOR 03
ORIGIN: DARK ARCHIVE ORIGINAL

01. THE FLOOR PLAN

The old building had a floor plan for every level. Offices, storage spaces and stairwells were marked in neat black lines. But on the third floor, one small rectangle had no label.

02. THE NIGHT SHIFT

A night-shift clerk noticed the blank space while checking the building records. The door was ordinary. There was no sign, no room number and no entry in the maintenance log. The clerk wrote one line in the notebook: “Room not listed.”

03. THE INVENTORY

The next morning, the facilities team checked the plans. They found an outdated drawing from years earlier, but it did not explain the blank rectangle. No one could confirm whether the space had ever been used.

04. THE SIMPLE POSSIBILITY

Old buildings are changed over time. Walls are moved, storage areas are sealed and plans become outdated. The room might have been a drafting error, a former cupboard or a space created during renovation.

05. FINAL RECORD

Nothing in the file proves anything supernatural. The mystery is smaller and stranger: a blank space in a document, waiting for someone to ask why it was there. This is a fictional story.
