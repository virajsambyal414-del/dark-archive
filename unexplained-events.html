UNEXPLAINED EVENTS

WHEN THE EVIDENCE IS REAL, BUT THE ANSWER IS STILL DEBATED

ARCHIVE CLASSIFICATION: MYSTERY / INVESTIGATION
SUBJECT: Historical events and open questions
STATUS: FACTUAL OVERVIEW
LOCATION: VARIOUS
ORIGIN: HISTORICAL RECORDS

01. THE TUNGUSKA EVENT

In 1908, a powerful explosion flattened trees across a large area near the Tunguska River in Siberia. No confirmed impact crater was found. The leading scientific explanation is that a small space object broke apart in the atmosphere, releasing energy above the ground.

02. THE WOW! SIGNAL

In 1977, a radio telescope in Ohio recorded a strong, narrowband radio signal that became known as the Wow! signal. It was detected once and has not been reliably repeated. Its exact source remains uncertain; it is not proof of extraterrestrial contact.

03. THE MARY CELESTE

The merchant ship Mary Celeste was found adrift in the Atlantic in 1872, with its crew missing. Investigators and historians have proposed several explanations, but the available evidence does not settle every detail.

04. HOW INVESTIGATORS WORK

Researchers compare eyewitness accounts, physical evidence, records, instruments and the conditions of the time. They test ordinary explanations first and clearly label what remains unknown.

05. UNKNOWN DOES NOT MEAN IMPOSSIBLE TO EXPLAIN

A gap in the record is not automatically evidence of something paranormal. New documents, better measurements or improved scientific methods can change what is understood.

06. FINAL RECORD

The most responsible archive keeps uncertainty visible. It records what happened, what evidence exists and which questions have not yet been answered.
